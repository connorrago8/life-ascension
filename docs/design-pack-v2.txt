GLOBAL ART DIRECTION
Dark fantasy × cyberpunk; detailed 16-bit pixel art; adult, realistic proportions. Charcoal and midnight glass; neon magenta #ff2bd6, cyan #00f0ff, aged gold #c9a227. No pastel, chibi, kawaii, or heart motifs. Pixel edges should remain crisp. All glow should be restrained and legible against dark backgrounds. Each piece must work as a modular layer without changing the base character silhouette unnecessarily. Cosmetics have no combat stats. Existing item identifiers and unlocks must be preserved; names below are display copy proposals.

5. STREAK SHIELD — PLAYER-FACING RULES AND COPY

PROPOSAL, NOT CONFIRMED EXISTING MECHANIC. Requires Connor's approval before implementation. Never silently change streak calculations.

Rules text: "Once each calendar month, a Streak Shield can protect your daily streak if you miss one qualifying game day. It keeps your streak from resetting, but it does not complete a quest, award XP, or increase your streak count for the missed day. Your next qualifying day continues from your last earned streak count. Unused shields do not roll over. A second missed day without a shield resets the streak."
Boundary: "A game day runs from 5:00 AM to 4:59 AM America/Chicago. The monthly allowance resets at the first 5:00 AM game-day boundary assigned to the new local calendar month; this exact interpretation needs product approval."
Available label: "Streak Shield ready · 1 this month"
Used label: "Streak Shield used · 0 remaining"
Activation: "Streak protected. You missed a day, but your journey continues. No XP was awarded."
Next-day nudge: "Your shield saved your streak. Complete a quest today to keep it going."
No shield remaining: "No shields left this month. Your next qualifying day starts a new streak."
Accessibility: Never rely on glow/color alone; always show the shield state in text.
