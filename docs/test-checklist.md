## Together

- [ ] Check that our XP totals remain separate
- [ ] Verify that completing a couple quest does not double-award XP
- [ ] Check mobile layout and navigation
- [ ] Confirm no unwanted pastel or cute styling
- [ ] Verify neither account can change the other’s equipment without authorization

## Activity XP rules

- Small is 5 XP, about 5–15 minutes. Medium is 10 XP, about 15–45 minutes. Big is 20 XP, about 45 minutes or more.
- A duration adjustment never goes above 20.
- One award per entry. The original description and game-day date stay as logged.
- Backdating is at most 30 days and never a future date.
- A manual tier override is checked on the server.
- Wearing a cosmetic or opening the app does not grant XP.
